FAQ

Questions? Answered.

What is VoxCard?

VoxCard is a premium physical smart business card by VoxluMedia. It's powered by the Voxlumedia Smart Chip, which connects your card to a personalized digital profile — your contact details, social profiles, website, booking link and more — that people open with a single tap.

What makes VoxCard different from traditional business cards?

A paper card is out of date the moment your details change. Your VoxCard connects to a living digital profile, so when you update your phone number, title, or website, people see the new information the next time they tap. No reprinting, no boxes of outdated cards.

What is the Voxlumedia Smart Chip?

It's the small chip built into every VoxCard product. It connects your physical card, keychain, or wristband to your personalized online profile. The chip itself doesn't store your details — it points to your live profile, which is why your updates show up without replacing the card.

How does the tap-to-share feature work?

The other person holds their compatible smartphone close to your VoxCard. Their phone shows a prompt, and your profile opens in their browser. From there they can call, email, visit your links, or save you straight to their contacts. You can also share your profile through its QR code or link.

Do recipients need to download an app?

No. Your profile opens directly in their phone's web browser. Nobody needs to install anything — not you, and not the people you meet.

Can I update my information after purchasing?

Yes, as often as you like. Edit your profile from your VoxCard dashboard and the changes appear the next time someone opens your card.

Will I need to buy another card if my phone number or job changes?

No. That's the whole point. Update your profile and keep using the same VoxCard.

What's the difference between the card, keychain, and wristband?

All three use the same Voxlumedia Smart Chip and connect to your VoxCard profile. The difference is how you carry it: the card fits in your wallet and hands over like a classic business card, the keychain lives on your keys, and the wristband is worn for hands-free networking at events.

Are black and white cards available?

Yes. The VoxCard Smart Business Card comes in Premium Black and Classic White. Choose your color when you add the card to your cart.

Which smartphones are compatible?

Tap-to-share works with smartphones that support contactless tag reading, which includes most recent iPhone and Android models. On some Android phones this setting needs to be switched on. Any phone with a camera can still open your profile through your QR code, and anyone can open your profile link.

How do I activate and personalize my VoxCard?

After your purchase, you'll follow a short onboarding process to create your VoxCard profile — add your photo, contact details, social links, and website — and connect it to your card. Once that's done, your card is ready to tap.

How long does shipping take?

VoxCard Smart Business Cards ship free within the US and arrive in 5–7 business days. Shipping details for the keychain and wristband are shown at checkout.

What happens if I lose my card?

Your information lives in your online VoxCard profile, not on the card, so nothing is lost. Contact us at sales@voxlumedia.com and we'll help you get a replacement connected to your profile.

Is there a recurring subscription?

No. VoxCard is a one-time purchase. The price you see is what you pay — your digital profile access is included with your product.
